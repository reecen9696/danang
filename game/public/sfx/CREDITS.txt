Gun audio: "Snake's Authentic Gun Sounds 2" by Snakepit Studios.

The files here are derived from that pack: leading silence trimmed so a shot
fires the instant you click, a 3 ms fade-in to kill the resulting click, and
re-encoded to 160 kbps MP3. Levels are untouched, so the relative loudness of
each calibre is the recordings' own. Regenerate with tools/prep-sfx.sh.

Weapon mapping (see src/audio/Audio.ts):
  Pistol    .22LR single, pitched down slightly
  SMG       5.56 single
  Rifle     7.62x54R single (Mosin)
  Shotgun   7.62x39 single, pitched down with a synthesised sub
  Launcher  7.62x54R, pitched well down with a synthesised sub
  Explosion 7.62x54R pitched far down, layered over the synthesised boom

Enemy voices: two Vietnamese takes (voice-a, voice-b), sliced at playback from
the offset table in src/audio/voiceLines.ts. Regenerate with tools/prep-voice.sh.

Death cries: death-cry-a, death-cry-b — two takes played at random when a bot
is killed, loudness-matched to the voice takes and trimmed head and tail so the
cry lands on the frame the man drops. Regenerate with tools/prep-death.sh.

Jungle ambience: "Tropical Jungle Birds" by placidplace (Pixabay, 108380).

ambience-jungle is a 60 s seamless loop cut from the ten-minute original: the
four seconds that follow the loop body are crossfaded back over its head, so
the end of the loop runs into audio that genuinely followed it in the
recording. Highpassed at 110 Hz to clear the field rumble, normalised to
-26 LUFS so it sits far under the gunfire, stereo at 96 kbps. It is decoded and
looped in the audio graph rather than streamed from an <audio> element, because
element looping is not gapless. Regenerate with tools/prep-ambience.sh.
